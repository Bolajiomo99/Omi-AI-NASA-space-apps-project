What Omi AI does

Bringing statistical clarity to environmental time-series records

Climate and weather spreadsheets often remain locked in raw CSV exports or complex scripts. Omi AI helps researchers, students, and regional planners inspect historical temperature and rainfall behaviour, run non-parametric trend tests, and interpret findings with an integrated research assistant.

Evidence before assumptions

Every chart and metric in the workspace is generated exclusively from connected regional data or uploaded CSV observations—never synthetic placeholders.

Transparent statistical diagnostics

Review observation counts, missing-data quality checks, mean/minimum/maximum bounds, and statistical significance alongside visual trend lines.

Built for Nigerian environmental research

Pre-configured study profiles for coastal Lagos State and forest-transition Edo State support rapid regional investigation and comparison.

How it works

From raw observations to interpretable regional insight

A disciplined four-step workflow designed for reproducible environmental exploration.

01

Select a region

Choose Lagos State, Edo State, or specify your own geographic study area inside the workspace.

02

Explore environmental data

03

Analyse trends

Understand results
